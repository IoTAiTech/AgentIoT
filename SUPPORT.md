## What to include in a public report

- Version from `/api/version` or the `VERSION` file
- Architecture (`x86_64` or `aarch64`)
- Steps to reproduce
- Expected versus actual behaviour

## What not to include

Passwords, tokens, private keys, contracts, internal architecture,
session identifiers, and fleet addresses.
